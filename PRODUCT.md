# KoBo Product Direction

## Register

Product UI first. KoBo is a working rental marketplace and owner workspace, not a campaign site. Public pages may explain trust, but authenticated flows optimize clarity, status, and action.

## Users

- Students and young workers seeking a kos near campus.
- Kos owners and managers operating one or more properties.

## Purpose

Help renters evaluate a kos, apply with clear expectations, and communicate with a real owner. Help owners screen applicants and operate rooms without surrendering payment control.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, practical, and locally familiar. Warm enough for a housing decision, restrained enough for money and identity. Preserve the existing teal and cyan system and Plus Jakarta Sans typography.

## Anti-references

- Ad-heavy listing portals with stale or ambiguous availability.
- Sterile hotel-booking language that ignores owner approval.
- Generic SaaS dashboards with decorative gradients, glass, or excessive cards.
- Payment claims that exceed what KoBo actually verifies.

## Design Principles

1. Show the real state. Distinguish application, approval, payment, and active tenancy.
2. Earn trust with names, faces, conditions, and plain explanations rather than badges alone.
3. Put the next action near the state it resolves.
4. Preserve familiar patterns and the current design system before inventing new components.
5. Keep mobile forms short and owner decisions scannable.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic labels and status messages, 40 px controls, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and layouts that remain usable at 390 px without horizontal scrolling.
